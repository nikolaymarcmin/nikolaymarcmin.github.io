Advisory

I work with founders, CEOs, and investors in the frontier technology companies, helping them build the financial intelligence their companies need to grow with clarity and purpose and maximize value they generate for the stakeholders.

Twenty years of finance experience across frontier technology companies has taught me that the strategic finance is not a back-office function — it is a strategic weapon if used correctly. The companies that win at the frontier are the ones that understand their economics deeply, allocate capital with discipline, and tell their financial story with conviction. I help build those capabilities.

Building Finance from Scratch

For companies that need a finance function before they can afford a full-time CFO. I design the infrastructure, build the core models, establish the foundational processes, and infuse finanical intelligence to support confident decision making.

Unit Economics & Pricing Strategy

For companies building frontier products with unique and complex unit economics. I help determine unit economics, build value proposition, and develop pricing architecture that captures appropriate share of the value generated by the product.

Path to Profitability

For companies navigating the transition from scaling to sustainable economics. I identify the key drivers of margin improvement, define the milestones that matter, determine capital requirments, and build the long-range financial plan that gives investors confidence in the path ahead.

Investor & Board Narratives

For companies preparing for fundraising or strategic transactions. I translate complex financial realities into clear, compelling narratives — distilling the economic logic of the business into the language investors and board members actually respond to.

Fractional CFO

For companies that need senior financial leadership on a part-time or project basis. I embed with your team, partner with your leadership, and provide the strategic finance capability of a seasoned CFO without the full-time commitment.
